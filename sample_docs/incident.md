# Incident Response Runbook
Severity 1 incidents (customer-facing outage) page the on-call engineer immediately and require an update every 30 minutes.
Severity 2 incidents (degraded performance) must be acknowledged within one hour.
Open an incident channel, assign an incident commander, and keep a timeline of actions.
After resolution, write a blameless postmortem within five business days that lists root cause and follow-up actions.
